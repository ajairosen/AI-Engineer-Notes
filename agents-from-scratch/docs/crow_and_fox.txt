Once upon a time, in a quiet forest, a hungry fox was wandering in search of food. He had been looking everywhere since morning, but he could not find anything to eat.

As he walked beneath the tall trees, he noticed a crow sitting on a branch. In the crow's beak was a large, delicious piece of cheese.

The fox immediately wanted the cheese, but he knew he could not climb the tree to take it. So, he decided to use his cleverness.

The fox looked up at the crow and said, “Good morning, dear Crow! What beautiful feathers you have! They shine like the finest silk.”

The crow was pleased to hear this.

The fox continued, “I have seen many birds, but none is as beautiful as you. Surely, your voice must be just as wonderful as your appearance. If you sing for me, I am certain you will be called the queen of all birds!”

The crow became very proud. She wanted to prove that she had a beautiful voice. Forgetting about the cheese in her beak, she opened her mouth and began to sing.

“Caw! Caw!”

The moment she opened her beak, the piece of cheese fell straight to the ground.

The fox quickly grabbed it and smiled.

“Thank you, dear Crow,” he said. “You may have beautiful feathers, but remember: do not trust everyone who praises you.”

With that, the fox ran away with the cheese, leaving the crow sitting sadly on the branch.

The crow realized that the fox had tricked her by using false praise. She felt foolish for allowing her pride to overcome her common sense.

From that day onward, the crow learned to be careful of people who flatter others only to gain something from them.
